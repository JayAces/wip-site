Your Rights

Privacy & Data Control

You control every aspect of your data. Every crisis you log has three sharing levels. You can change these settings anytime. You can withdraw from the Project entirely. No questions asked.

Private

Only You See It

Your crisis log is yours alone. Researchers cannot access it. The Project can only use aggregated, de-identified patterns to detect community-wide trends.

De-Identified

Researchers Can Use It

Approved researchers can access your data for analysis. Your name stays hidden. You decide what fields they can see.

Public

Warriors, Researchers, Everyone

Your story can inspire and inform. Public submissions feed the dashboard, research publications, and institutional briefings, with your consent to share.

You're Never Trapped

You can delete your data anytime. You can change sharing levels on individual submissions. You can withdraw consent for specific research projects. Your privacy controls are in your hands.

What happens to my Signal Journal if I delete my account?

Your journal settings and every saved version of your plans are deleted. If you rated any reflections, only a de-identified record of each rating is kept: which tracker factors it was about, the rating you gave, and the date. Your account is detached from it and the text you were shown is removed, so it cannot be linked back to you. It is kept so the Project can check the journal is working safely. Your crisis reports are separate: you can request their removal from the dashboard at any time.

How We Work

Terms & Commitments

The Project is built on mutual respect. Warriors own their data. Researchers must earn trust. Institutions must follow Warriors' rules. Here's what that means in practice.

Warriors Own the Data

You submit data. You control it. You decide who sees it. You can delete it. Institutional partners never own your crisis logs. They borrow access with your permission.

Researchers Earn Access

Research requires IRB approval, Governance Council approval, and written commitments to Warriors. No data access without these checkpoints. Researchers must acknowledge Warriors in publications and share findings before they publish.

No Selling, No Surveilling

Your data is never sold. Your data is never shared with insurers, employers, or marketing firms. The Project is not a surveillance tool. It is evidence infrastructure built by Warriors, for Warriors.

You Control Consent

Your initial consent covers crisis logging. Every research project that uses your data requires a separate approval decision from you. You can grant, deny, or revoke access per project.

Our Principles

Data Ethics & Community Benefit

Warriors' data generates real institutional change. Research findings get published. Hospitals change protocols. Policy makers see evidence they've never seen. The Project's ethics are designed to ensure Warriors benefit from the data they create.

Transparency Every research request is public. Warriors see who's asking for data and why. No hidden research agendas.
Accountability Researchers who misuse data lose access. Institutions that exploit Warriors face permanent ban. Governance Council holds everyone to the same standard.
Benefit-Sharing Warriors see results. When your data leads to a published finding or policy change, you're told about it. You can request co-authorship on research you contributed to.
Agency You're never just a data point. Warriors participate in research design. You vote on governance. Your voice shapes how the Project evolves.

The Bottom Line

Warriors' data is evidence infrastructure. It exists to strengthen institutions AND to shift power back to Warriors. That dual commitment is what makes the Project different, and what makes it worthy of your trust.
