Quick ER Card

What to say and ask for, built from Warrior data. Screenshot it. Read it in the parking lot.

Say This First
At triage

"I have sickle cell disease, hemoglobin SS. I'm in a vaso-occlusive crisis right now. My pain is currently [number] out of 10. This started [timeframe] after [trigger, if known]. I'd like to talk about a treatment protocol that's worked for me before."

Vaso-occlusive crisis is the clinical term for a pain crisis caused by blocked blood flow. Using it signals you know your body and your condition.

If Your Pain Isn't Being Addressed

"I want this reflected in my chart: my pain remains at [number] and I don't feel it's been adequately managed. I'd like to speak with someone about adjusting the protocol."

Before Any Discharge
Pain still high

"My pain is still [number] out of 10 and I don't feel it's adequately controlled. Can you document my concern and explain the follow-up plan?"

Before You Walk In
  • Know your baseline: your normal pain level and typical crisis presentation
  • Name your effective protocol if one has worked before
  • Bring a support person if you can, to document and advocate
  • Write down names and times of everyone you speak with
What You Can Ask For
  • A medical screening exam, regardless of ability to pay
  • Your pain assessed, and reassessed
  • A hematology consult, and the answer noted in your chart
  • Pain management decisions documented in writing

A request is not a guarantee. These are the words that help you be heard and documented, not a promise of outcome. Save this before your next crisis, not during it.
